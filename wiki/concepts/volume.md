# 銷量（Volume）

## 一句話
銷量 = 賣出去「多少東西」（幾箱、幾件、幾片），不管賣多少錢。

## 白話解釋
- 銷量是最「誠實」的需求訊號：漲價可以讓營收變大，但如果顧客買的件數變少，代表需求其實在流失。
- 公司常同時報兩種口徑：消費者實際買走的量，和公司出貨給通路的量，兩者不一定同步。

## 各家怎麼說（證據）
- KO：Q4 單位箱數（unit case）成長 1%。 {{ko-q4-2025: "we grew organic revenues 5%. Unit case growth was 1%."}}
- KO：2025 全年銷量持平，但 Q4 每個月都在改善。 {{ko-q4-2025: "While unit case volume was flat in 2025, we ended the year with better momentum as volume improved each month during the fourth quarter."}}
- KO：出貨給裝瓶廠的「濃縮液銷量」跟單位箱數可以差 3 點，原因是出貨時間點。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Concentrate sales grew 3 points ahead of unit cases, driven primarily by the timing of concentrate shipments and an extra day in the quarter."}}
- KO：管理層指出要靠印度等長期銷量貢獻者回升。 {{ko-q4-2025: "India is a long-term contributor of volume growth, so that needs to build back."}}
- PG：這季銷量 −1 點。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- PG：北美銷量 −3 點，其中約 2 點是基期因素。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales in North America were down 2%. Volume was down three points, including a roughly two-point headwind from the base period trade inventory impacts I mentioned."}}
- KMB：已連續第 8 季維持 volume + mix 的穩健表現。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "delivering an eighth consecutive quarter of solid volume plus mix performance in Q4"}}
- KMB：管理層把銷量成長歸因於每一個價格階梯都有說得通的價值主張。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "we're really focused on serving consumers on every rung with a compelling value proposition. I think that's the reason why we're growing volumes right now."}}
- KHC：目標是「以銷量帶動」的獲利成長。 {{khc-q4-2025: "to be delivering volume-led profitable growth"}}

## 全年數字與定義（批次 2）
- PG 對「有機銷量」的定義：單位銷量的年增，排除併購出售與一次性項目。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Organic volume growth reflects year-over-year changes in unit volume excluding the impacts of acquisitions and divestitures and certain one-time items, if applicable, and is used to explain changes in organic sales."}}
- KMB 2025 全年：有機銷售 +1.7%，主要是銷量 +2.5%。 {{kmb-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales increased 1.7% driven by volume gains of 2.5%, partially offset by lower pricing."}}
- KO 2025 全年：銷量 +1%。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}}
- KHC 2025 全年：volume/mix 不利 4.1 個百分點，是有機淨銷售下滑的主因。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 3.4% to $24.9 billion in 2025 compared to $25.8 billion in 2024, primarily due to the unfavorable volume/mix (4.1 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.7 pp)."}}

## 容易誤會
- 銷量成長 ≠ 營收成長：營收還要看價格與組合。見 [[concepts/price]]、[[concepts/mix]]。
- 單季銷量會被出貨時間與基期干擾，見 [[concepts/timing-and-base-effects]]。
