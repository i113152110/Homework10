# 組合（Mix）

## 一句話
組合 = 「賣的東西結構變了」：貴的賣得多還是便宜的賣得多？大包裝還是小包裝？哪個通路、哪個地區賣得多？

## 白話解釋
- 即使每個商品的價錢都沒變，只要顧客改買比較便宜的品項或較大的包裝，平均每件賣的錢就會下降——這是 mix 變差。
- mix 變差不一定是壞事：有時是顧客轉去成長更快的通路或大包裝，銷量反而增加。

## 品牌商的 mix（產品、包裝、通路、地區）
- KO：Q4 組合拖累 3 點，原因是「事業組合、品類組合與時間點」的特殊組合。 {{ko-q4-2025: "offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix, which was driven by an unusual combination of business mix, category mix and timing of a number of items"}}
- KO：價格 4 點減掉組合 3 點，得到 price/mix 1 點。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Our price/mix growth of 1% was primarily driven by approximately 4 points of pricing actions, offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix"}} <!--calc: 4-3=1-->
- PG：這季組合持平。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- KMB：管理層把 price/mix 的變化拆成三件事，其中一件是 club 通路的大包裝。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "there's really three things going on there on the price mix"}} {{kmb-q4-2025: "they're buying larger pack sizes at a lower price per unit. So you see more volume, but a little bit of a drag on pricing"}}
- KMB 對外只報 volume + mix，並連續第 8 季維持穩健。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "delivering an eighth consecutive quarter of solid volume plus mix performance in Q4"}}

## 10-K 的正式定義（批次 2）
- KO 對 price/mix 的定義：訂價行動，加上品類、產品、包裝、通路、地區的組合。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "refers to the change in net operating revenues caused by factors such as pricing actions taken by the Company and, where applicable, our bottling partners; the mix of categories, products and packages sold; and the mix of channels and geographic territories where the sales occurred"}}
- **price/mix 是倒推的剩餘值**：總變動扣掉銷量、匯率、併購出售之後剩下的部分。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "The impact of price/mix is calculated by subtracting the change in net operating revenues resulting from volume increases or decreases, fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, and acquisitions and divestitures from the total change in net operating revenues."}}
- 代回 KO 2025 全年：營收 +2%，扣掉銷量 +1%、匯率 −2%、併購出售 −1%，剩下 price/mix +4%。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}} <!--calc: 2-1+2+1=4-->
- PG 的表格把 mix 獨立成一欄，與 volume、price 並列。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Volume with Acquisitions & Divestitures Volume Excluding Acquisitions & Divestitures Foreign Exchange Price Mix Other Net Sales Growth"}}
- KHC 的表格把 mix 跟銷量綁成 Volume/Mix，跟價格分開。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales Price Volume/Mix 2025 Compared to 2024"}}

## 零售商的 mix（事業組合）
- 沃爾瑪講的 mix 是「廣告、會員這類高毛利事業」占比變高，不是商品包裝。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As eCommerce drives the majority of our sales growth, we are improving eCommerce economics with increased contributions from business mix, most notably in higher-margin areas like advertising and membership fees."}}
- 詳見 [[concepts/retail-business-mix]]。

## 容易誤會
- 同一個詞 mix，品牌商與零售商指的東西不同，不能直接比較。
- 各家把 mix 跟誰綁在一起報也不同（Price/Mix、Volume/Mix 或單獨報），見 [[concepts/reporting-conventions]]。
