# 各家報法不同

## 一句話
同樣叫「成長」，各家公司切法不一樣：有人把 price 與 mix 綁在一起、有人把 volume 與 mix 綁在一起、有人三項分開報。**看到 mix 時，第一個問題是：它跟誰綁在一起？**

## 白話解釋
- 如果直接把 A 公司的 price/mix 拿去跟 B 公司的 price 比，會得到錯誤結論，因為兩者包含的東西不一樣。
- 這是本 Wiki 最重要的發現之一：它來自 10-K 的表格欄位，而不是法說會的口頭說法。

## 四種報法（10-K 證據）
- KO：銷量、price/mix、匯率、併購出售四項；price/mix 是倒推的剩餘值。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "we generally consider the following factors: (1) volume growth (concentrate sales volume or unit case volume, as applicable); (2) changes in price/mix; (3) foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations; and (4) acquisitions and divestitures"}}
- KHC：Price 與 Volume/Mix 兩項，mix 跟銷量綁在一起。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales Price Volume/Mix 2025 Compared to 2024"}}
- PG：volume、匯率、price、mix、other 五欄，價格與組合分開。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Volume with Acquisitions & Divestitures Volume Excluding Acquisitions & Divestitures Foreign Exchange Price Mix Other Net Sales Growth"}}
- KMB：敘述上用「銷量」與「較低的價格（與組合）」說明。 {{kmb-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales increased 1.8% primarily from volume gains of 2.6%, with all categories growing volume, partially offset by lower pricing and mix."}}

## 法說會的報法（批次 1 證據）
- KO：price/mix 合併成一項。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Our price/mix growth of 1% was primarily driven by approximately 4 points of pricing actions, offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix"}}
- PG：銷量、價格、組合三項分開報。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- KMB：把 volume 與 mix 綁在一起談。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "delivering an eighth consecutive quarter of solid volume plus mix performance in Q4"}}
- WMT：零售商用固定匯率口徑，另外談事業組合。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "revenue is up 4.9% in constant currency, including growth in eCommerce of 24%. Adjusted operating income grew even faster at 10.5%."}}

## 為什麼不能直接比
- 例：KO 全年 price/mix 是 +4%（**包含**組合），KHC 全年價格是 +0.7 個百分點（**不含**組合，組合在 volume/mix 裡）——兩個數字不能直接比大小。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}} {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 3.4% to $24.9 billion in 2025 compared to $25.8 billion in 2024, primarily due to the unfavorable volume/mix (4.1 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.7 pp)."}}

## 容易誤會
- 「mix 拖累」可能是 price/mix 的一部分（KO），也可能被併入 volume/mix（KHC）。
- 公司報告的是「管理層選擇揭露的口徑」，不是會計準則規定的分類。
