# 單季雜訊與基期效應

## 一句話
單季數字常被「出貨時間、多一天少一天、去年同期特別高」扭曲，所以管理層才會一直說：看趨勢，不要只看一季。

## 白話解釋
- 基期（base period）就是拿來比較的「去年同期」。去年同期如果特別好（例如大家囤貨），今年同期就會顯得很差，即使今年的真實需求沒變。
- 出貨量（賣給通路）和消費量（顧客真正買走）之間有時間差，所以會有「出貨提前或延後」的雜訊。

## 各家怎麼說（證據）
- KO CEO 的建議：看價格與組合時，用四個季度的視角。這是 KO 管理層的個人說法，不是通用規則。 {{ko-q4-2025: "always try and take a four quarter view"}}
- KO：Q4 底層價格約 4%，被組合拖累成 1%。 {{ko-q4-2025: "In the fourth quarter, pricing came in at 1%, but actually it was really 4%."}}
- KO CEO 的簡化看法：把組合的單季波動拿掉，Q4 就是底層價格 4% 加銷量 1%，約等於 5% 的營收成長。 {{ko-q4-2025: "If you take that, what you see is 4% underlying price and 1% volume. So you see the fourth quarter in simple terms as a 5% revenue growth quarter"}} <!--calc: 4+1=5-->
- KO：濃縮液出貨比單位箱數多 3 點，是出貨時間與多一天造成的。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Concentrate sales grew 3 points ahead of unit cases, driven primarily by the timing of concentrate shipments and an extra day in the quarter."}}
- KO：2026 年第一季多 6 天，但約一半的好處會被出貨週期抵銷。 {{ko-q4-2025: "due to a calendar shift in the first quarter, while we'll have six additional days, we expect approximately half of the benefit to be offset by concentrate shipments cycling and timing"}}
- PG：去年同期有囤貨（港口罷工、颶風），讓這季的基期偏高。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "the base period included trade and consumer pantry loading, driven by port strikes and hurricanes in early October"}}
- PG：北美銷量 −3 點中，約 2 點來自基期因素。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales in North America were down 2%. Volume was down three points, including a roughly two-point headwind from the base period trade inventory impacts I mentioned."}}
- PG：Family Care 下降約 10%，主要是基期。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "family care was down approximately 10%, primarily due to the base period dynamics we described"}}
- PG：管理層事前就說這會是全年最弱的一季。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "We anticipated this would be the softest quarter of the fiscal year and we remain confident in stronger growth in the back half."}}

## 容易誤會
- 「基期」不是壞事或好事，只是比較基準；要問「去年同期有沒有一次性因素」。
- 管理層會用基期替自己解釋，投資人仍應對照下一季是否真的回升。
