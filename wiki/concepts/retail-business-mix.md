# 零售商的事業組合

## 一句話
零售商講的 mix，常常不是「賣哪種商品」，而是「錢從哪幾種事業賺進來」：廣告、會員費這類高毛利收入占比變高，獲利就會比營收成長更快。

## 白話解釋
- 賣商品的毛利通常很薄；廣告（讓品牌商付錢曝光）與會員費（顧客付年費）的毛利高很多。
- 所以零售商的「事業組合」改善，是獲利成長快過營收成長的主要原因之一。

## 各家怎麼說（證據）
- WMT：營業利益 +10.5%，約為營收成長的兩倍。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "revenue is up 4.9% in constant currency, including growth in eCommerce of 24%. Adjusted operating income grew even faster at 10.5%."}}
- WMT：第一個原因是事業組合：廣告與會員費占比提高。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As eCommerce drives the majority of our sales growth, we are improving eCommerce economics with increased contributions from business mix, most notably in higher-margin areas like advertising and membership fees."}}
- WMT：全球廣告事業 Q4 成長 37%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Our advertising businesses globally increased 37%"}}
- WMT：廣告收入加會員費，已占本季營業利益將近三分之一。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "the combination of advertising income and membership fees represented nearly one-third of our operating income this quarter"}}
- WMT：全年廣告事業成長 46%，達 6.4 billion 美元。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Our advertising businesses globally were up 46% this year to $6.4 billion"}}
- WMT：存貨成長只有銷售成長的一半左右，代表庫存周轉更有效率。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Inventory increased 2.6% in constant currency, or approximately half the rate of sales growth for the full year."}}

- WMT 10-K：把「類別與事業組合」列為改善營業利益率的工具之一。 {{wmt-10k-fy2026: "Margin - improve our operating income margin through productivity initiatives as well as category and business mix"}}

## 容易誤會
- 這裡的 mix 與品牌商的 mix（產品、包裝、通路）是兩回事，見 [[concepts/mix]]。
- 事業組合改善會提高「利潤率」，但不會讓「銷量」變多。
