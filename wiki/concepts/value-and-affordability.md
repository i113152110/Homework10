# 價值導向與負擔得起

## 一句話
消費者變得更會比價，公司因此調整「開口價、包裝大小、價格階梯」，這直接改變 price 與 mix。

## 白話解釋
- 當顧客覺得東西太貴，公司有三種反應：降低入門款價格（開口價）、改包裝大小、增加促銷。這些動作都會同時影響價格和組合。
- 這也是為什麼「單看價格」會看不清楚：公司可能主動讓價格或組合變差，來換取銷量。

## 各家怎麼說（證據）
- KMB：用「好、更好、最好」價格階梯，每一階都提供有競爭力的產品。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "we started to focus on delivering superior propositions at what we said is every rung of the good, better, best ladder"}}
- KMB：預期消費者壓力與對價值的重視會持續。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "We expect pressure on the consumer and a focus on value to persist."}}
- KO：在亞太調整價格／包裝架構，聚焦絕對價格帶。 {{ko-q4-2025: "tailoring our brand price/pack architecture with a focus on attractive absolute price points and value offerings"}}
- KO：北美出現低收入消費者壓力。 {{ko-q4-2025: "despite continued macroeconomic pressure on lower-income consumers"}}
- KHC：增加的投資約一半用在價格、產品、包裝。 {{khc-q4-2025: "about half of this would be against price and product and packaging, improving the way we show up for our consumers at store"}}
- KHC：包含「對的開口價」。 {{khc-q4-2025: "half of that being, as I said, against the brands and showing up for consumers with the right opening price points"}}
- PG：通膨讓消費者重新評估價值。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Inflation across food, energy, health care, and many other areas of spending has taken a toll on consumers, and how they assess value."}}
- WMT：用降價（rollbacks、EDLP）壓低食品價格，讓顧客有餘力買一般商品。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As we lean into lower prices through rollbacks and EDLP in grocery categories, we're helping customers unlock purchasing power for general merchandise."}}
- WMT：市占成長主要來自年收入超過 100,000 美元的家庭。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "the majority of our share gains came from households making more than $100,000"}}

## 容易誤會
- 「價值導向」不等於「全面降價」：多數公司是調整階梯與包裝，而不是每個品項都降。
- 不同收入層的行為不一樣，同一家公司可能同時看到高收入顧客成長、低收入顧客吃緊。
