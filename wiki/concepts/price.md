# 價格（Price）

## 一句話
價格 = 「同一個東西」賣的價錢變了多少（漲價、降價、促銷深淺都算）。

## 白話解釋
- 價格是最容易被看見、也最容易被誤解的成長來源：漲價能立刻推高營收，但如果顧客因此少買，銷量會抵銷它。
- 包裝尺寸、促銷檔期、開口價（入門款的價格）都會改變顧客實際付的價錢。

## 各家怎麼說（證據）
- KO：Q4 約 4 點的營收成長來自漲價。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Our price/mix growth of 1% was primarily driven by approximately 4 points of pricing actions, offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix"}}
- KO：CEO 強調底層價格其實是 4%，單季 1% 是被組合拖累後的數字。 {{ko-q4-2025: "In the fourth quarter, pricing came in at 1%, but actually it was really 4%."}}
- PG：這季價格 +1 點。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- KMB：管理層說自己的促銷仍低於品類與 2019 年水準。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "our end market promotional activity for the year remains below the category and 2019 levels"}}
- KMB：在 club 通路，大包裝的單位價格較低，會讓「價格」指標被拖累，但銷量增加。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "they're buying larger pack sizes at a lower price per unit. So you see more volume, but a little bit of a drag on pricing"}}
- KHC：把增加的投資用在「對的開口價」與價格、產品、包裝。 {{khc-q4-2025: "half of that being, as I said, against the brands and showing up for consumers with the right opening price points"}}
- KHC：訂價時盯著原物料成本、維持紀律。 {{khc-q4-2025: "very disciplined about following the commodity"}}

## 全年數字與成因（批次 2）
- KHC 2025 全年：價格 +0.7 個百分點，仍不足以抵銷 volume/mix 的下滑。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 3.4% to $24.9 billion in 2025 compared to $25.8 billion in 2024, primarily due to the unfavorable volume/mix (4.1 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.7 pp)."}}
- KHC 的漲價是為了抵銷投入成本（主要是咖啡），所以「價格」不一定代表需求強。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Higher pricing was taken in certain categories to mitigate higher input costs, primarily in coffee."}}
- KMB 2025 全年：價格較低，部分抵銷了銷量成長。 {{kmb-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales increased 1.7% driven by volume gains of 2.5%, partially offset by lower pricing."}}
- KO 把價格與組合合併成 price/mix 一項；2025 全年是 +4%。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}}

## 容易誤會
- 法說會報的「價格」那一欄不是通膨率：它只是營收成長的其中一塊。
- 大包裝讓單位價格下降，不等於公司降價。這是組合效應，見 [[concepts/mix]]。
