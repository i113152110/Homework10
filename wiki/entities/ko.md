# 可口可樂（KO）

## 一句話
飲料品牌商，把成長拆成「銷量（單位箱數）」與「price/mix」，是本 Wiki 示範 price/mix 的主要範例。

## 它怎麼談成長
- Q4 有機營收 +5%，銷量 +1%，price/mix +1%（價格約 4 點、組合 −3 點）。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Our price/mix growth of 1% was primarily driven by approximately 4 points of pricing actions, offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix"}} {{ko-q4-2025: "we grew organic revenues 5%. Unit case growth was 1%."}}
- CEO 提醒看四季，而不是單季。 {{ko-q4-2025: "always try and take a four quarter view"}}
- CEO 的簡化看法（Q4）：把組合的單季波動拿掉，Q4 就是底層價格 4% 加銷量 1%，約等於 5% 的營收成長。 {{ko-q4-2025: "If you take that, what you see is 4% underlying price and 1% volume. So you see the fourth quarter in simple terms as a 5% revenue growth quarter"}} <!--calc: 4+1=5-->
- 2026 指引：有機營收 +4% 到 5%，期望價量約 50/50。 {{ko-q4-2025: "We expect organic revenue growth of 4% to 5%, which is in line with our long-term growth algorithm."}} {{ko-q4-2025: "we believe we will get back to a balance, so call it 50/50"}}
- 連續 19 季取得價值市占。 {{ko-q4-2025: "We also continued our streak of gaining value share for the last 19 quarters."}}

- 2025 全年（10-K）：銷量 +1%、price/mix +4%、匯率 −2%、併購出售 −1%，合計營收 +2%。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}}
