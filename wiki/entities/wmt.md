# 沃爾瑪（WMT）

## 一句話
零售商（不是品牌商）。它的「mix」是事業組合：廣告與會員收入占比提高，讓獲利成長快過營收。

## 它怎麼談成長
- Q4 營收 +4.9%、eCommerce +24%、營業利益 +10.5%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "revenue is up 4.9% in constant currency, including growth in eCommerce of 24%. Adjusted operating income grew even faster at 10.5%."}}
- 廣告加會員費已占本季營業利益將近三分之一。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "the combination of advertising income and membership fees represented nearly one-third of our operating income this quarter"}}
- 用降價（rollbacks、EDLP）壓低食品價格，帶動一般商品。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As we lean into lower prices through rollbacks and EDLP in grocery categories, we're helping customers unlock purchasing power for general merchandise."}}
- FY2027 指引：銷售 +3.5% 到 4.5%，營業利益 +6% 到 8%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Full year constant currency sales are expected to grow between 3.5% and 4.5%. And operating income is expected to grow between 6% and 8%"}}

- 10-K：把「類別與事業組合」列為改善利潤率的工具之一。 {{wmt-10k-fy2026: "Margin - improve our operating income margin through productivity initiatives as well as category and business mix"}}
