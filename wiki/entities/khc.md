# 卡夫亨氏（KHC）

## 一句話
食品品牌商（番茄醬、起司、通心粉等），正處於「先把品牌投資補回來、再談分拆」的階段。

## 它怎麼談成長
- 暫停分拆，把資源放在回到有機成長。 {{khc-q4-2025: "that led to the decision to pause the spin because we want to put 100% of our focus"}}
- 600 million 美元投資約一半用在價格、產品、包裝。 {{khc-q4-2025: "the $600 million, I think, gives us a lot of confidence"}} {{khc-q4-2025: "about half of this would be against price and product and packaging, improving the way we show up for our consumers at store"}}
- 目標是「以銷量帶動」的獲利成長。 {{khc-q4-2025: "to be delivering volume-led profitable growth"}}

- 2025 全年（10-K）：有機淨銷售 −3.4%，volume/mix −4.1 個百分點、價格 +0.7 個百分點。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 3.4% to $24.9 billion in 2025 compared to $25.8 billion in 2024, primarily due to the unfavorable volume/mix (4.1 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.7 pp)."}}
- 北美漲價主要是為了抵銷咖啡等投入成本。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Higher pricing was taken in certain categories to mitigate higher input costs, primarily in coffee."}}
