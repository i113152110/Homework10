# 金百利克拉克（KMB）

## 一句話
衛生紙、尿布等個人護理品牌商，對外強調「volume + mix」，並用價格階梯同時守住高階與平價市場。

## 它怎麼談成長
- 連續第 8 季維持 volume + mix 的穩健表現。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "delivering an eighth consecutive quarter of solid volume plus mix performance in Q4"}}
- Q4 volume/mix +1.7%，兩年疊加 +3.6%。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "In Q4, as you saw from our results, our volume mix was up 1.7%. But on a two-year stack basis in Q4, it was up 3.6%."}}
- 用「好、更好、最好」階梯服務不同價位的消費者。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "we started to focus on delivering superior propositions at what we said is every rung of the good, better, best ladder"}}
- club 通路的大包裝讓價格被拖累、銷量增加。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "they're buying larger pack sizes at a lower price per unit. So you see more volume, but a little bit of a drag on pricing"}}

- 2025 全年（10-K）：有機銷售 +1.7%，主要是銷量 +2.5%，被較低的價格抵銷。 {{kmb-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales increased 1.7% driven by volume gains of 2.5%, partially offset by lower pricing."}}
