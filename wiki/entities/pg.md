# 寶僑（PG）

## 一句話
日用品品牌商（洗衣、嬰兒、美容等），把銷量、價格、組合三項分開報，並常用「基期」解釋單季表現。

## 它怎麼談成長
- FY2026 Q2：有機銷售持平，銷量 −1、價格 +1、組合持平。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- 北美銷量被去年同期的囤貨基期拖累約 2 點。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales in North America were down 2%. Volume was down three points, including a roughly two-point headwind from the base period trade inventory impacts I mentioned."}}
- 全年指引：有機銷售持平到 +4%。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "we continue to expect organic sales growth of in line to plus 4%"}}
- 預期關稅增加約 500 million 美元的稅前成本，同時靠生產力改善支應再投資。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "approximately $500 million before tax and higher costs from tariffs"}} {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Strong productivity improvement of 270 basis points with healthy reinvestment in innovation, and demand creation."}}

- FY2025 全年（10-K）：有機銷售 +2%（排除併購出售與匯率）。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales, which exclude the impact of acquisitions and divestitures and foreign exchange, increased 2%."}}
