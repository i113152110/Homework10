# 寶僑（PG）FY2025 10-K（只 ingest 與營收拆解有關的 MD&A 段落）

**一句話**：給出「有機銷售」與「有機銷量」的正式定義，並用 volume / 匯率 / price / mix / other 五欄拆解淨銷售成長。
**來源品質**：SEC 文件；表格含大量「—」與括號負數，抽字後難以逐格引用，因此只引用定義與敘述句。

## 可引用重點
- 有機銷售的定義：排除併購出售與匯率的影響；FY2025 有機銷售 +2%。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales, which exclude the impact of acquisitions and divestitures and foreign exchange, increased 2%."}}
- 有機銷量的定義：單位銷量的年增，排除併購出售與一次性項目，用來解釋有機銷售的變化。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Organic volume growth reflects year-over-year changes in unit volume excluding the impacts of acquisitions and divestitures and certain one-time items, if applicable, and is used to explain changes in organic sales."}}
- 報法：表格欄位是 volume、匯率、price、mix、other 五欄，價格與組合分開。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Volume with Acquisitions & Divestitures Volume Excluding Acquisitions & Divestitures Foreign Exchange Price Mix Other Net Sales Growth"}}
- 「Other」欄包含併購出售的組合影響與四捨五入，用來讓各欄對得上淨銷售。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Other includes the sales mix impact from acquisitions and divestitures and rounding impacts necessary to reconcile volume to net sales."}}
- 長期成長目標的第一項：有機銷售成長高於所在市場。 {{pg-10k-fy2025: "Organic sales growth above market growth rates in the categories and geographies in which we compete"}}
