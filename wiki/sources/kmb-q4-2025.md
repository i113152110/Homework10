# 金百利克拉克（KMB）2025 Q4 法說會

**一句話**：KMB 只強調「volume + mix」，並在 Q&A 回答分析師「price/mix 為什麼在減速」——這是理解「mix 變差不一定是壞事」的好材料。
**來源品質**：Motley Fool 逐字稿，開頭缺財務長的 prepared remarks，內容以 Q&A 為主；數字有些轉錄雜訊（例如把年度縮寫誤轉成百分比），只引用語意清楚的句子。

## 可引用重點
- 已連續第 8 季維持 volume + mix 的穩健表現。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "delivering an eighth consecutive quarter of solid volume plus mix performance in Q4"}}
- Q4 volume/mix 成長 1.7%；以兩年疊加計是 3.6%。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "In Q4, as you saw from our results, our volume mix was up 1.7%. But on a two-year stack basis in Q4, it was up 3.6%."}}
- 面對「price/mix 減速」的提問，管理層說原因有三個。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "there's really three things going on there on the price mix"}}
- 原因之一是通路組合：消費者在 club 通路買較大包裝、單位價格較低，所以量增加但價格被拖累。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "they're buying larger pack sizes at a lower price per unit. So you see more volume, but a little bit of a drag on pricing"}}
- 原因之二是促銷與創新的節奏；管理層強調自己的促銷仍低於品類與 2019 年水準。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "our end market promotional activity for the year remains below the category and 2019 levels"}}
- 策略上用「好、更好、最好」價格階梯，在每一階都提供有競爭力的產品。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "we started to focus on delivering superior propositions at what we said is every rung of the good, better, best ladder"}}
- 管理層認為銷量成長的原因是每一階都有說得通的價值主張。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "we're really focused on serving consumers on every rung with a compelling value proposition. I think that's the reason why we're growing volumes right now."}}
- 預期消費者壓力與對價值的重視會持續。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "We expect pressure on the consumer and a focus on value to persist."}}
- 一家大型 club 通路客戶不再給品牌獨家，KMB 預期部分尿布鋪貨損失。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "major club player has moved away from... branded exclusivity in our category"}}
- 這個鋪貨損失已計入全年展望，影響約 60 個基點。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "it's a headwind of around 60 basis points for the full year"}}
- 管理層的展望隱含全球品類成長約 2%。 {{kmb-q4-2025: "our category outlook is around 2% globally, plus or minus"}}
