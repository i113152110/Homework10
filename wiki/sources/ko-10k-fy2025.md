# 可口可樂（KO）FY2025 10-K（只 ingest 與營收拆解有關的 MD&A 段落）

**一句話**：給出 price/mix 的**正式定義與算法**，還有 2025 全年的「銷量 / price-mix / 匯率 / 併購出售」拆解表。
**來源品質**：SEC 文件，格式乾淨；表格抽字後以「一列文字」呈現，因此引用整列而不是單一格子。

## 可引用重點
- KO 分析營收時會看四個因素：銷量、price/mix、匯率、併購出售。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "we generally consider the following factors: (1) volume growth (concentrate sales volume or unit case volume, as applicable); (2) changes in price/mix; (3) foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations; and (4) acquisitions and divestitures"}}
- price/mix 的定義：公司（與裝瓶夥伴）的訂價行動，加上品類、產品、包裝、通路、地區的組合。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "refers to the change in net operating revenues caused by factors such as pricing actions taken by the Company and, where applicable, our bottling partners; the mix of categories, products and packages sold; and the mix of channels and geographic territories where the sales occurred"}}
- price/mix 是「倒推的剩餘值」：用總變動扣掉銷量、匯率、併購出售的影響。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "The impact of price/mix is calculated by subtracting the change in net operating revenues resulting from volume increases or decreases, fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, and acquisitions and divestitures from the total change in net operating revenues."}}
- 2025 全年：銷量 +1%、price/mix +4%、匯率 −2%、併購出售 −1%，合計營收 +2%。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Volume Price/Mix Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Fluctuations Acquisitions & Divestitures Total Consolidated 1% 4% (2)% (1)% 2%"}} <!--calc: 1+4-2-1=2-->
- 公司說明提供 price/mix 是為了讓投資人理解這些因素合在一起的影響。 {{ko-10k-fy2025: "Management believes that providing investors with price/mix enhances their understanding about the combined impact that these items had on the Company's net operating revenues."}}
