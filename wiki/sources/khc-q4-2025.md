# 卡夫亨氏（KHC）2025 Q4 法說會

**一句話**：新任 CEO 宣布暫停分拆、把資源砸在品牌上，Q&A 裡清楚說明增加的投資要花在哪（價格、產品、包裝）。
**來源品質**：Motley Fool 逐字稿，**只有 Q&A，沒有 prepared remarks**；有轉錄錯字（例如把 taste elevation 轉成 data elevation）。這份來源**不拿來抓財務數字**，數字改用 KHC 10-K（批次 2）。

## 可引用重點
- 為了把全部資源放在讓公司回到有機成長，管理層決定暫停分拆。 {{khc-q4-2025: "that led to the decision to pause the spin because we want to put 100% of our focus"}}
- 公司提到的 600 million 美元投資，約一半用在價格、產品與包裝。 {{khc-q4-2025: "the $600 million, I think, gives us a lot of confidence"}} {{khc-q4-2025: "about half of this would be against price and product and packaging, improving the way we show up for our consumers at store"}}
- 其中一部分是回到「對的開口價（opening price points）」與促銷。 {{khc-q4-2025: "half of that being, as I said, against the brands and showing up for consumers with the right opening price points"}}
- 目標是「以銷量帶動的獲利成長」，而不只是靠漲價。 {{khc-q4-2025: "to be delivering volume-led profitable growth"}}
- 管理層說 70% 的營收已在拿回市占（逐字稿把 taste elevation 誤轉成 data elevation）。 {{khc-q4-2025: "70% of the revenue in data elevation is now gaining market share"}} {{khc-q4-2025: "we are seeing good momentum on our taste elevation portfolio worldwide"}}
- 支出會從第二季開始加大，管理層希望下半年看到趨勢轉向。 {{khc-q4-2025: "you're going to see the spend really start to ramp up in the second quarter"}}
- 不是每個品牌都會成長：重點放在「對投資有反應」的品牌。 {{khc-q4-2025: "you're never gonna be in a situation where every brand is growing"}}
- 商品成本面維持定價紀律。 {{khc-q4-2025: "very disciplined about following the commodity"}}
