# 卡夫亨氏（KHC）FY2025 10-K（只 ingest 與營收拆解有關的 MD&A 段落）

**一句話**：KHC 把成長拆成 **Price** 與 **Volume/Mix** 兩項——mix 跟銷量綁在一起，和 KO 的 price/mix 剛好相反。
**來源品質**：SEC 文件，格式乾淨。補足批次 1 中 KHC 逐字稿沒有數字的缺口。

## 可引用重點
- 報法：表格欄位是 Price 與 Volume/Mix。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales Price Volume/Mix 2025 Compared to 2024"}}
- 全年有機淨銷售 −3.4%：volume/mix 不利 4.1 個百分點，價格 +0.7 個百分點仍不足以抵銷。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 3.4% to $24.9 billion in 2025 compared to $25.8 billion in 2024, primarily due to the unfavorable volume/mix (4.1 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.7 pp)."}} <!--calc: 0.7-4.1=-3.4-->
- 北美：有機淨銷售 −4.7%，volume/mix 不利 5.0 個百分點，價格 +0.3 個百分點。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Organic Net Sales decreased 4.7% to $18.6 billion in 2025 compared to $19.5 billion in 2024, primarily due to unfavorable volume/mix (5.0 pp), which more than offset higher pricing (0.3 pp)."}}
- 北美 volume/mix 不利的原因是冷盤肉、咖啡、部分調味醬等品類下滑。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Unfavorable volume/mix was primarily due to declines in cold cuts, coffee, certain condiments, bacon, frozen snacks, and desserts."}}
- 部分品類漲價是為了抵銷較高的投入成本，主要是咖啡。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Higher pricing was taken in certain categories to mitigate higher input costs, primarily in coffee."}}
- 新興市場的 volume/mix 是正的，北美與已開發國際市場是負的。 {{khc-10k-fy2025: "Volume/mix in North America and International Developed Markets was unfavorable, while volume/mix in Emerging Markets was favorable."}}
