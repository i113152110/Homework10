# 可口可樂（KO）2025 Q4 法說會

**一句話**：CEO 交接前的最後一場法說會；財務長把成長拆成「銷量」與「price/mix」，CEO 在 Q&A 裡示範為什麼不能只看單季的 price/mix。
**來源品質**：FactSet 逐字稿，格式乾淨，同時有 prepared remarks 與 Q&A。

## 可引用重點
- Q4 有機營收（organic revenue）成長 5%，單位箱數（unit case，可當作銷量）成長 1%。 {{ko-q4-2025: "we grew organic revenues 5%. Unit case growth was 1%."}}
- Q4 的 price/mix 只有 1%：約 4 點來自漲價，被 3 點不利的組合抵銷。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Our price/mix growth of 1% was primarily driven by approximately 4 points of pricing actions, offset by 3 points of unfavorable mix"}} <!--calc: 4-3=1-->
- CEO 說：單季的 1% 其實是 4% 的底層價格被組合拖累，所以要看四個季度。 {{ko-q4-2025: "In the fourth quarter, pricing came in at 1%, but actually it was really 4%."}} {{ko-q4-2025: "always try and take a four quarter view"}}
- CEO 的簡化看法：把組合的單季波動拿掉，Q4 就是底層價格 4% 加銷量 1%，約等於 5% 的營收成長。 {{ko-q4-2025: "If you take that, what you see is 4% underlying price and 1% volume. So you see the fourth quarter in simple terms as a 5% revenue growth quarter"}} <!--calc: 4+1=5-->
- 2026 年指引：有機營收成長 4% 到 5%。 {{ko-q4-2025: "We expect organic revenue growth of 4% to 5%, which is in line with our long-term growth algorithm."}}
- 管理層預期價與量會回到「約 50/50」的平衡。 {{ko-q4-2025: "we believe we will get back to a balance, so call it 50/50"}}
- 2025 全年銷量持平，但 Q4 每個月都在改善。 {{ko-q4-2025: "While unit case volume was flat in 2025, we ended the year with better momentum as volume improved each month during the fourth quarter."}}
- 已連續 19 季取得價值市占（value share，以金額計的市占）。 {{ko-q4-2025: "We also continued our streak of gaining value share for the last 19 quarters."}}
- 濃縮液出貨量比單位箱數多成長 3 點，原因是出貨時間點與多一天，不是需求突然變好。 {{ko-q4-2025: "Concentrate sales grew 3 points ahead of unit cases, driven primarily by the timing of concentrate shipments and an extra day in the quarter."}}
- 成本面：管理層認為整體成本籃子的影響可控。 {{ko-q4-2025: "we expect the overall impact on our cost basket to be manageable"}}
- 銷量要靠印度等「長期銷量貢獻者」回升。 {{ko-q4-2025: "India is a long-term contributor of volume growth, so that needs to build back."}}
- 在亞太用「價格／包裝架構」與「絕對價格帶」因應消費疲軟。 {{ko-q4-2025: "tailoring our brand price/pack architecture with a focus on attractive absolute price points and value offerings"}}
- 北美出現低收入消費者壓力，但仍是量與價值市占都成長。 {{ko-q4-2025: "despite continued macroeconomic pressure on lower-income consumers"}}
