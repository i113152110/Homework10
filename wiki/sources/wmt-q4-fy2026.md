# 沃爾瑪（WMT）FY2026 Q4 法說會

**一句話**：零售商也談 mix，但指的是「事業組合」——廣告與會員收入占比變高，讓獲利成長比營收成長快。
**來源品質**：FactSet 逐字稿，格式乾淨，同時有 prepared remarks 與 Q&A。

## 可引用重點
- Q4 營收（固定匯率）+4.9%，eCommerce +24%，調整後營業利益 +10.5%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "revenue is up 4.9% in constant currency, including growth in eCommerce of 24%. Adjusted operating income grew even faster at 10.5%."}}
- 獲利成長的第一個原因是「事業組合」：eCommerce 帶動成長，同時廣告與會員費這類高毛利收入占比提高。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As eCommerce drives the majority of our sales growth, we are improving eCommerce economics with increased contributions from business mix, most notably in higher-margin areas like advertising and membership fees."}}
- 全球廣告事業 Q4 成長 37%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Our advertising businesses globally increased 37%"}}
- 合併的會員收入成長超過 15%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Consolidated membership income increased more than 15%"}}
- 廣告收入加會員費，已占本季營業利益將近三分之一。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "the combination of advertising income and membership fees represented nearly one-third of our operating income this quarter"}}
- 存貨管理：存貨成長約為銷售成長的一半（全年 2.6%）。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Inventory increased 2.6% in constant currency, or approximately half the rate of sales growth for the full year."}}
- 「商品類別組合」：降價（rollbacks、EDLP）壓低食品價格，讓顧客有餘力買一般商品。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "As we lean into lower prices through rollbacks and EDLP in grocery categories, we're helping customers unlock purchasing power for general merchandise."}}
- 市占成長主要來自年收入超過 100,000 美元的家庭；低收入家庭的荷包仍吃緊。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "the majority of our share gains came from households making more than $100,000"}} {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "For households earning below $50,000, we continue to see that wallets are stretched"}}
- FY2027 指引：固定匯率銷售成長 3.5% 到 4.5%，營業利益 6% 到 8%。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Full year constant currency sales are expected to grow between 3.5% and 4.5%. And operating income is expected to grow between 6% and 8%"}}
- 全年廣告事業成長 46%，達 6.4 billion 美元。 {{wmt-q4-fy2026: "Our advertising businesses globally were up 46% this year to $6.4 billion"}}
