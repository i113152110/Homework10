# 寶僑（P&G）FY2026 Q2 法說會

**一句話**：P&G 把「銷量、價格、組合」三項**分開**報，並用「基期」解釋為什麼這季看起來特別弱。
**來源品質**：Motley Fool 逐字稿，有少量轉錄錯字（例如把 Global market share 轉成 Global Exhibit），因此只引用語意清楚的句子。

## 可引用重點
- 這季有機銷售與去年持平：銷量 −1 點、價格 +1 點、組合持平。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales were in line with the prior year. Volume was down one point, pricing up a point, and mix was flat for the quarter."}}
- 北美 organic sales −2%，銷量 −3 點，其中約 2 點是去年同期囤貨（基期）造成。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Organic sales in North America were down 2%. Volume was down three points, including a roughly two-point headwind from the base period trade inventory impacts I mentioned."}}
- 基期之所以高：去年同期有通路與消費者因港口罷工、颶風而囤貨。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "the base period included trade and consumer pantry loading, driven by port strikes and hurricanes in early October"}}
- 受影響最大的類別 Family Care 下降約 10%，主因就是基期。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "family care was down approximately 10%, primarily due to the base period dynamics we described"}}
- 管理層早就預期這是全年最弱的一季。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "We anticipated this would be the softest quarter of the fiscal year and we remain confident in stronger growth in the back half."}}
- 全年有機銷售指引維持「持平到 +4%」。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "we continue to expect organic sales growth of in line to plus 4%"}}
- 管理層估計 P&G 所在市場的成長約 2%（以金額計）。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Global market growth for our portfolio footprint is around 2%, on a value basis"}}
- 關稅：預期增加約 500 million 美元的稅前成本。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "approximately $500 million before tax and higher costs from tariffs"}}
- 生產力改善 270 個基點，用來支應創新與需求創造的再投資。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Strong productivity improvement of 270 basis points with healthy reinvestment in innovation, and demand creation."}}
- 通膨讓消費者重新評估「值不值得」。 {{pg-q2-fy2026: "Inflation across food, energy, health care, and many other areas of spending has taken a toll on consumers, and how they assess value."}}
